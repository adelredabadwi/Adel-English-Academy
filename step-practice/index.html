Instructions
  1. Answer 20 Listening, 40 Reading, and 40 Grammar questions in that order.
  2. Select one answer (A, B, C, or D) for each question; you can change it before submitting.
  3. Listening uses synthetic browser text-to-speech audio. Use Play, Pause, and Replay; transcripts stay hidden until you submit.
  4. Use Previous, Next, the question navigator, and Flag for Review as you work.
  5. Your answers and test deadline are saved in this browser if local storage is available. Leaving or closing the page does not stop the countdown.
  6. Submission is automatic when the 120-minute deadline passes. After submission, you can view explanations and print or save your report as a PDF.

Scores are for practice only, not official STEP score conversions or validated proficiency levels. A standalone HTML file is not secure for supervised examinations, and results are not sent to the teacher.
